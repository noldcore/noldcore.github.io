4. Purposes and Legal Bases

NoldCore relies on the following legal bases, depending on the feature and context:

  • Contract performance — to provide Worldcoil, retain local progress, deliver and restore in-app purchases, and provide requested support.
  • Consent — for Firebase Analytics and for advertising processing where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — for basic security, fraud prevention, debugging and business administration where permitted by law.

Consent withdrawal

You can change your UMP advertising and analytics choices at any time in Worldcoil: open Settings, then Privacy Settings. You can also contact NoldCore about privacy requests.

If you withdraw analytics consent, Worldcoil disables future Firebase Analytics collection. Previously collected data is not automatically deleted; contact NoldCore to request deletion where applicable. Advertising choices may change the ads shown or whether ad requests are permitted.

5. Local Saves

Local save games

Worldcoil stores progress, unlocked levels, scores, settings and purchase entitlements locally on your device. The app has no NoldCore account or Unity Cloud Save integration. UMP stores your privacy choices locally for use by its consent flow.

Local game data generally remains until you delete the app or clear its data, subject to device or platform backups. Purchased entitlements can be restored from the relevant platform store where available.

Where data is stored

Worldcoil does not upload its save game to a NoldCore or Unity cloud save service. The device operating system may separately back up app data under your platform settings.

6. Interstitial & Rewarded Ads, AdMob and Firebase Analytics

Interstitial & Rewarded Ads / Google AdMob

Worldcoil may show interstitial ads between gameplay sessions and offers an optional rewarded ad to continue a run. The continue reward is granted only after the rewarded ad reports a successful reward. The ad-free purchase removes interstitial ads, but rewarded ads remain optional.

Ads may be provided by Google AdMob and its advertising partners. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the availability of identifiers, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • Personalized ads may use identifiers, approximate location, app activity, ad interactions, and other signals to select ads that may be more relevant to you.
  • Non-personalized ads are selected using more limited signals such as approximate location, app context, language, and basic device information, but may still use identifiers for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and measurement where allowed.
  • Ad impressions and interactions, including interstitial ads and rewarded ad completion data, may be processed for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and granting an in-game reward after a completed rewarded ad.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy

Firebase Analytics

Worldcoil uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure usage and improve gameplay and features, but collection is disabled by default and starts only if you explicitly grant the analytics option in the Google UMP privacy message.

Firebase may process an app instance identifier, device and app information, approximate region, the Android advertising ID where available and permitted, and automatic app/session events. Worldcoil sends level starts, selections and unlocks; run score, duration, end reason and continue count; rewarded-continue offers and outcomes; and shop opens. Worldcoil does not send names, email addresses, payment card details, raw purchase receipts or purchase tokens as custom analytics parameters. It does not send its own purchase-revenue or ad-impression events; automatic events from linked Google services may still appear after consent. Any future use of the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) requires a separate iOS tracking decision and, where required, Apple's tracking permission.

Firebase privacy information: Privacy and Security in Firebase

7. Consent and Privacy Settings

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays the applicable privacy message for your region and records your choices. The published EU message offers consent, refusal and an option to manage choices; a US state message may offer different choices. Worldcoil requests ads only when UMP reports that ad requests are permitted. Refusing personalized ads does not necessarily prevent all advertising: Google may still serve limited ads where permitted.

Firebase Analytics is off by default. Worldcoil enables it only when UMP's analytics_storage value is explicitly granted. A missing, refused or unreadable analytics choice keeps it off. At each app start and after you change UMP choices, Worldcoil rechecks the choice and disables future analytics collection when consent is withdrawn. Open Settings > Privacy Settings to review or change the available choices. Depending on region and platform, these may include:

  • Accept or reject advertising consent and manage advertising purposes and partners.
  • Grant or refuse analytics storage, which controls Firebase Analytics collection in Worldcoil.
  • Reset or restrict your device advertising identifier.
  • Request access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, or portability of applicable personal data.

Some processing that is strictly necessary for security, fraud prevention, purchase delivery, app functionality, or legal compliance may continue even if optional consent is withdrawn.

8. In-App Purchases

Apple App Store and Google Play

Worldcoil currently offers two non-consumable purchases on Android through Google Play: Level Pack 01 and Ad Free. The level pack unlocks five premium levels. Ad Free suppresses interstitial ads but not optional rewarded ads. Apple App Store purchases would apply only if a future iOS version is released and its products are configured.

Unity IAP passes product and transaction information, including a receipt or purchase token, to Worldcoil for local purchase validation. Worldcoil stores the product and entitlement plus a SHA-256 hash of the verified transaction identifier in its local save; raw receipts, tokens and JWS data are not stored there or logged by Worldcoil. Store services remain responsible for payment and account data.

No own payment card processing

NoldCore does not directly collect, store, or process your payment card number, bank account details, Apple ID password, Google account password, or comparable store payment credentials.

Refunds, chargebacks, payment authentication, store-collected taxes and billing support are handled by the relevant store under its own terms and privacy policy.

10. Sharing, International Transfers and Retention

Sharing

Worldcoil uses the providers listed above to serve permitted ads, process consent, collect opted-in analytics, deliver purchases and respond to support. GitHub hosts this informational website. The game does not use Unity Cloud Save.

NoldCore does not sell your direct contact details to data brokers.

International transfers

Data may be processed in Germany, the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, and other countries where NoldCore, platform providers, or service providers operate.

Where required, NoldCore relies on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, data processing agreements, or comparable legal mechanisms.

Website hosting (GitHub Pages)

NoldCore's informational website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. NoldCore does not collect visitor information through its own website analytics or maintain its own visitor database. However, GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are accessed. For details on GitHub's processing and retention, please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention

NoldCore keeps personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Examples: local saves generally remain on the device until the user deletes them or removes the app, subject to device and platform backup behavior; support emails are retained as needed to process the request and any applicable legal obligations. Purchase-related information, where received by NoldCore, is kept only as needed to deliver and restore purchases or meet applicable legal obligations.

Where third-party services such as Google, Apple or Unity independently process data, their applicable retention rules also apply. Their retention periods depend on the relevant service and configuration. For processing controlled by NoldCore, retention is determined by the purpose of processing and applicable legal retention requirements.
